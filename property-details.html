For Sale Luxury Villa Premium Location 5 Bedrooms New Construction

Property Overview

This exquisite luxury villa in the prestigious Buch Villas area of Multan represents the pinnacle of modern architecture and sophisticated living. Spread over 10 marlas of prime land, this property offers a perfect blend of contemporary design and functional elegance.

The villa features a stunning exterior with Italian marble finishes, large French windows, and a beautifully landscaped garden with a water fountain. The interior has been designed with meticulous attention to detail, featuring high ceilings, imported marble flooring, and premium fixtures throughout.

Key Features

  • Spacious living room with double-height ceiling
  • Modern modular kitchen with breakfast counter
  • 5 bedrooms with attached dressing areas
  • 4 modern bathrooms with imported fittings
  • Central air conditioning throughout
  • Solar water heating system
  • 24/7 security with CCTV surveillance
  • Backup power generator
