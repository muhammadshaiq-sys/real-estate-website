Our Story

From humble beginnings to industry leadership

A Legacy of Excellence

Founded in 1998 by Muhammad Taimur, Elite Properties began with a single development project and a vision to transform the real estate industry. What started as a small family business has grown into a multinational real estate development and investment firm.

Over the past 25 years, we've successfully completed over 50 projects, delivering exceptional value to our investors and creating landmark properties that define skylines.

Our journey is marked by strategic expansion, innovative design, and unwavering commitment to quality. Today, we manage a $78.5 Million portfolio while maintaining the personal touch and attention to detail that defined our early years.
